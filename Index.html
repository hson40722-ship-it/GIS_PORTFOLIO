01 About Me

I'm an undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in GIS & Remote Sensing at Ardhi University in Tanzania. My studies combine geography, mapping, satellite image interpretation and spatial data analysis.

I'm especially interested in spatial analysis, digital cartography, geospatial programming, photogrammetry, web GIS and Earth observation. I enjoy the process of taking raw spatial data and turning it into something people can understand and act on.

I'm still early in my journey and learning with every project. My goal is to grow into a professional GIS and Remote Sensing specialist, building skills in geospatial data science and sharing my work openly as I go.
